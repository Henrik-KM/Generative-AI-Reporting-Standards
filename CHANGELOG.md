# Changelog

We recommend semantic versioning: `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`.

- **MAJOR**: changes that could change checklist interpretation (IDs added, removed, or materially redefined).
- **MINOR**: backward compatible additions or clarifications.
- **PATCH**: typo fixes and purely editorial changes.

## v1.0.0-draft (2026-10-08, development branch only)

- Distinguished mandatory category responses from mandatory performance of every named operation; allowed justified inapplicability in the development format.
- Added explicit `not_performed` responses and operational details with values, source locations and rationales for gaps.
- Moved inference throughput to generation and separated retained-candidate counts from evaluator calls and failures.
- Made baseline requirements follow the comparison claim and clarified molecular benchmark applicability alongside inorganic options.
- Added dynamical-stability reporting and concrete shortcut and perturbation examples.
- Added an oxygen-carrier author-format example, with a directly verified initial database and explicit gaps in later-run provenance. Differences between publication accounts remain visible rather than resolved through assumed values.
- Required operational details for development author records and aligned their missing-value and applicability rules in the schema and dependency-free validator.
- Retained 0.2.0 retrospective records and their original validation semantics. No public release or DOI update was made.

## v0.2.0 (2026-08-28)

- Aligned the checklist with the revised manuscript: nine required items, three context-dependent items, and one optional key-number summary.
- Made the leakage audit, inorganic feasibility criteria, and inorganic baseline requirements operational.
- Required uncertainty across independent runs when stochastic variation affects a claim, without prescribing a fixed number of runs for every study.
- Clarified that governance controls are context dependent and should be reported only when a material concern exists.
- Added a JSON Schema, a dependency-free validator, and three sourced worked examples.
- Corrected text-encoding artefacts and updated the author-response templates.

## v0.1.0 (2026-05-21)

- Initial public draft of the minimum reporting checklist.
